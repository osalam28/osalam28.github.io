How I work

An operating system based on four principles, that guides how I approach product leadership

Decision systems that protect teams

Clear decision rights, prioritization rules, and lightweight governance where appropriate, so that work aligns to outcomes and tradeoffs happen early.

Discovery earns the build (and budget)

Structured discovery, that frames what the focus is and validates risks before code: problem clarity, value hypothesis, and tests that reduce rework.

Measurement isn't optional

Feedback loops built into every delivery so teams learn quickly and can prove what changed because they shipped.

AI built with purpose and restraint

AI applied starting with the problem, not the tech and where it improves customer outcomes: recommendations, support, trust/fraud; all measured, governed and human-safe.
