STRATEGIC GROWTH PILLARS

PILLAR 01 — OPERATIONS

DYNAMIC VELOCITY

Demand shaping Platform unity
KPIs to track:
  • • refunds/cancels
  • • order error rate
  • • guest satisfaction
Surfaces touched:
  • • kiosk
  • • app/web
  • • digital menu boards/signage
  • • BOH/KDS signals

Goal: Make CX easy for Team Members while protecting speed and accuracy during peak hours.

"Use real-time signals to dynamically steer digital ordering toward what restaurants can execute fast and accurately."

Opportunities:
  • Roll out demand shaping across Kiosk + App/Web using Byte by Yum! as the execution layer.
  • Test capacity-aware merchandising: highlighting "fast picks" to guide guests toward high-margin, low-complexity items during peak times.
  • Reduce preventable defects by syncing availability, mods, and kitchen load with what guests see on the front-end.
PILLAR 02 — DATA

CONTEXTUAL IQ

Unified CDP Loyalty
KPIs to track:
  • • digital revenue per active user
  • • incremental lift vs control
  • • frequency/repeat lift
Surfaces touched:
  • • Martech/CRM
  • • App/Web
  • • Kiosk
  • • Delivery Entry Points
  • • Loyalty Touchpoints (Identity)

Goal: Increase conversion and LTV through measurable, real-time personalization.

"Move from broad segmentation to context-driven decisions that proves incremental lift—not just redemption."

Opportunities:
  • Unify identity and behavior signals with Loyalty/CRM into a working CDP/decisioning layer (validate → scale).
  • Trigger offers based on daypart, location, intent, and situational context—then test for incrementality.
